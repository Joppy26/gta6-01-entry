DEL RIO — Sun-Baked Getaway is an original open-world crime game: Marisol "Sol" Vega, a getaway driver back in her hometown after five years away, works six story missions across a sun-baked border river city — terracotta roofs, white stucco, the turquoise Rio Seco, three bridges, a downtown skyline, a riverwalk and a canyon highway. Steal and drive cars, outrun the Del Rio police through a 5-star wanted system with chases and roadblocks, fight on foot with fists and a pistol, pull a toll-bridge heist, run taxi fares, hit stunt jumps, collect sun discs, snap photos at three viewpoints, and tune the radio between two procedural stations while the sun never leaves. Free roam, garage, paint shop and saves included.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move and drive, E to enter a car (or take a photo at a 📷 spot on foot), F to punch, Space to jump, R for radio, M to mute, Esc to pause.
